## 3. One Pain Point I Can Solve

### Contractors are losing jobs because they can't answer the phone

The problem, in plain words: Plumbers, electricians, HVAC techs, and similar trades are usually elbow-deep in a job — under a sink, up a ladder, next to loud equipment — and simply can't answer when a new customer calls. Data from over 1,200 contractors shows **62% of calls to contractors go unanswered**, the average small contractor misses **2-4 calls a week**, and **78% of callers won't leave a voicemail** — they just call the next plumber on Google instead. That's estimated to cost a small contracting business **$45,000-$120,000 a year** in lost jobs. Half a dozen different companies are independently writing near-identical "you're losing money to missed calls" blog posts right now, which is itself a sign this is a widely felt, recurring complaint.

Why this pain exists: A one-person or small-crew trade business has no receptionist — the owner is the technician, salesperson, and biller all at once. Voicemail doesn't help because someone calling about a leaking pipe wants an answer right now, not a callback tomorrow. A human answering service costs $200-$7,000 a month, which is out of reach for a business doing a handful of jobs a week, so most owners just quietly eat the lost revenue.

How to solve it with n8n + Claude (no phone-call AI needed, just text):

- **Trigger:** The instant a call is missed, the phone system (Google Voice, OpenPhone, or Twilio) sends a signal into n8n.

- **Step 1:** n8n immediately texts the caller: "Sorry we missed your call! This is [Business Name]'s assistant — what can we help with today?"

- **Step 2:** As the customer replies by text, Claude has a short back-and-forth to find out the job type, address, and how urgent it is.

- **Step 3:** Claude turns that into a clean summary; n8n saves it to a spreadsheet or CRM and texts the owner: "New lead: Maria, 45 Oak St, water heater leak, wants same-day service."

- **Step 4:** n8n texts the customer a booking link so the job can get scheduled without the owner ever picking up the phone.

Who to sell this to and what to charge: Solo and small-crew home-service businesses (plumbing, HVAC, electrical, garage doors, pest control, landscaping) with 1-15 employees who feel this pain but can't justify a $500+/month human answering service. A fair price based on similar micro-automation services: **$500-$1,500 one-time setup** plus **$150-$300/month** for the ongoing texting and Claude usage — a fraction of what they're already losing, and a fraction of what a live answering service costs.
